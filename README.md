# 偷桃夜 / Peach Heist Night

Two first-person scavenge-and-extract expeditions, selected from the main menu. In level I you play 猴儿 in a night pan-tao orchard: gather peaches, avoid the three fairies, and reach the extract shrine.

主界面可选择两个独立的第一人称搜寻撤离关卡。第一关你扮演猴儿，在夜间蟠桃园里捡桃、躲开三位仙子，抵达撤离点。

Explore unmarked forest clearings, branching passages and six enterable Chinese courtyard halls. Hold **E / left mouse** (or the touch action button) to pick peaches or search a chest. Releasing the action cancels searching. Each chest pays once per night; its loot only banks after extraction, and ancient loot can wake the hunt. The lantern reveals nearby rooms and trees; switching it off sharply reduces visible range. There are no painted paths, compass markers or zone labels. Camera height stays steady, and all moving monsters have directional footsteps that grow louder nearby.

穿行于无路标的林间空地、岔道与六座可进入的古式院落。长按 **E / 鼠标左键**（手机使用动作键）摘桃或搜箱，松开即中断搜寻。每箱每夜只可搜取一次，逃出后收获方可入袋；古老秘藏也可能唤醒追猎。灯笼照出近处堂屋与树木，熄灯后视野显著缩短。地图没有铺设路径、罗盘标记或区域标签。移动镜头保持稳定，所有移动中的怪物都有越近越响的方位脚步声。

## Level II · Greywall / 第二关 · 灰墙情报

Explore an original medieval walled city with16 enterable houses,18 supply caches, red roofs, towers, tall leafy trees and connected streets/alleys. Five enemy families patrol, see/hear you, chase around walls, search and disengage. Giant heights are4/6/8/12m, alongside1.7m ordinary wardens. Q / Space / right mouse (touch sword button) counterstrikes; E / left mouse (touch blueprint button) searches caches. Find four rarity tiers of Intel Blueprints worth10/100/1,000/10,000 and reach the far signal gate to extract. Pause/failure/extraction screens return to level selection.

第二关为原创西式石城：16栋可进入建筑、18个物资点、红瓦屋顶、城墙塔楼、高大树木和相连街巷。五类怪物具有巡逻、视听感知、绕墙追击、搜索与脱战行为。普通怪物高1.7米，巨人依次为4/6/8/12米。Q / 空格 / 右键（手机剑按钮）反击，E / 左键（手机图纸按钮）搜索。四档情报图纸价值分别为10/100/1,000/10,000，到远端信号城门撤离才能结算。暂停、失败和撤离后均可返回选关。
